Charter Life demo, build 0.38

Install the Android app

Download CharterLife-demo.apk
To install
  1. Open this page on your Android phone and tap the button.
  2. When the download finishes, tap the file (Downloads, or the notification).
  3. The first time, Android asks you to allow installs from your browser. Allow it, then tap Install.
It is not on the Play Store, so Android shows a one-time "unknown app" warning. Your saves stay on the phone and survive updates.
No install?

You can also just play it in the browser and use Add to Home screen.
