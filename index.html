Charter Life

Run an offshore fishing charter. Fish the marina and the shoals, upgrade your boat, and make the captain your own.

Play now
Works in your browser. Install it below to get the home-screen icon and play offline.

Install on Android

  1. Tap Play now in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install on the pill at the bottom of the game, or the browser menu (⋮) then Install app.
  3. Open Charter Life from your home screen.

Install on iPhone or iPad

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open Charter Life from your home screen.

On a computer

Just click Play now. In Chrome or Edge you can also use the install icon in the address bar to make it a desktop app.

In the demo

  • Two fishing regions: the marina and the shoals
  • Fight and land fish with the reel minigame
  • Boats, gear, a crew, and character customization
  • Progress up to level 12
Your progress saves on your device. The first launch needs internet; after that it works offline.
